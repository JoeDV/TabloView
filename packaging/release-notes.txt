TV view: big screens for a TV's browser (TV Bro on Google TV, Silk on Fire TV) used with the remote, with Continue watching, Recordings, Live TV and the Watch list (Device → Appearance, or add /tv to your TabloView address). Also a picture shape per channel (Auto, 4:3, 16:9 or Zoom) for stations that send a squeezed or stretched picture, streams nobody watches any more closed after 10 minutes, a folder picker that opens in front, security updates, and fixes for quick skips leaving streams running and the L key.
