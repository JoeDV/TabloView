More channels: add channels from outside your Tablo (Settings → Playback → More channels) and watch them in Live TV under More, on the PC and in the TV view. They don't use a Tablo tuner and can't be recorded. One click sets up Tubi's free channels with a full guide (a volunteer keeps that list, so Tubi's channels can change from day to day), or iptv-org's larger list, or your own playlist. Star or hide More channels, and use Remind me to be told when a show starts. A new optional switch lets your own devices on Tailscale skip the PIN. Fixed: captions on PCs with AMD graphics, some streams that never started playing, and a shared wrong-PIN lockout for Tailscale Funnel visitors.
