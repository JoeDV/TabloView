Settings has a new look, with its sections listed on the left, one row per setting and a search that finds any setting; Help is laid out the same way. On a TV you can now watch two live channels at once. Settings → About & diagnostics saves everything you've set up in one file, to move TabloView to a new PC. A notice tells you when your Tablo's drive is almost full and which watched recordings take the most space. The Skip break message can be put away while you keep watching, and Store copies say when a new version is waiting for the Store's approval. Fixed: a weather alert banner taken for the network logo, Find again doing nothing on recordings already checked, and live TV not starting after a recording was left stopped on a spot.
