TabloView now works with Plex: sign in under Settings → Plex and each new export shows up in Plex right away, marked watched if you watched it, with a wrong show match fixed. Show pages mark what Plex has already, and Delete once in Plex can clear a show's recordings from your Tablo once Plex has them. Exports carry their title, show, season, episode and air date for Plex, Jellyfin and Kodi, can go in show and season folders with .nfo files, and the export folder can be a network drive picked with Windows' own folder picker. Also new: Customize Home, full show controls from the Guide and Scheduled, Skip commercials before anything has recorded, and recording status in TV view. The Device page is now Settings. Fixed: recordings deleted by another app stayed in the Library, and finished recordings could keep saying Recording.
