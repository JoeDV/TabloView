Watch your antenna channels on a Roku, in VLC or Kodi, or on a smart TV: TabloView now shows up on your network as a media server (Settings → Live TV). Live TV → More keeps several playlists, each with its own favorites, hidden channels and group order, and lets you arrange the groups. A new Playback switch lets a recording play on after you skip or click the timeline. TabloView can also run in Docker on a Linux home server (DOCKER.md). Fixed: garbled sound after jumping around a recording in Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers.
