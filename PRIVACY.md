# TabloView privacy policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

TabloView ("TabloView for TabloTV" in the Microsoft Store) is an unofficial desktop app for Tablo DVRs, made by Joe Del Vecchio. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tablo or its owners.

**In short: TabloView doesn't collect anything about you. Nothing is sent to the developer.**

## What TabloView handles, and where it goes

- **Your Tablo account email and password.** When you sign in, they are sent only to Tablo's own sign-in service, the same one Tablo's apps use, to get access to your Tablo. Your password is never saved. Your email and the device access Tablo returns are stored on your computer so you stay signed in.
- **Your recordings, guide and settings.** TabloView reads these from your Tablo on your home network and from Tablo's guide service, and keeps a cache on your computer so pages load quickly. Changes you make (recording rules, deletions, watched status and resume points) are sent to your Tablo.
- **Video.** Recordings and live TV are streamed from your Tablo and converted on your computer for playback in your browser. Finding commercial breaks also happens on your computer. MP4 exports are saved to your Downloads folder, and a calendar file of your upcoming recordings is only created when you download it.
- **Update checks.** The downloaded version reads a small public file on GitHub listing the latest version number. The Microsoft Store version asks the Microsoft Store, through Windows, whether an update is waiting. Nothing about you is sent beyond what any web request includes, such as your IP address.

TabloView has no analytics, ads, tracking or crash reporting, and the developer runs no servers that receive your data.

## Where your data is stored

Everything TabloView keeps stays on your computer, in its app data folder (shown under **Device → About**). Signing out removes your Tablo access. Uninstalling a Microsoft Store copy removes its app data. For the downloaded version, delete `%LOCALAPPDATA%\TabloView` after uninstalling.

By default TabloView can only be opened from the computer it runs on. If you turn on **Network access**, other devices on your home network can open it after entering the PIN you choose. The PIN is stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value.

## Third parties

Your Tablo account and device are covered by Tablo's own privacy policy. TabloView shares nothing with anyone else.

## Children

TabloView is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
