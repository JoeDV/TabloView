Getting started

Install TabloView on a PC on the same home network as your Tablo, sign in with your Tablo account, and your recordings appear within a minute or two.

Windows

  • Microsoft Store (easiest): search for TabloView, or use the Store link. It updates itself.
  • Installer: download TabloView-Setup-<version>.exe from GitHub. It isn't code-signed, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.

TabloView then runs in the system tray (bottom right of the taskbar) and opens in your browser.

Mac and Linux

Download the Mac zip or Linux file from GitHub and follow MAC.md or LINUX.md in the folder. In short: install Python 3.12 and FFmpeg once, then start TabloView with its start script. Keep that Terminal window open while you use it.

Signing in

  1. Sign in with the email and password of your Tablo account. Your password goes only to Tablo's sign-in service and is never saved.
  2. If you have more than one Tablo, pick the one to use.
  3. The first sync loads your recordings, then the TV guide. Recordings and Live TV work right away; the Guide and Scheduled fill in as the guide loads.

After that, TabloView keeps itself in sync with your Tablo every 15 minutes, and watched status and resume points match the Tablo's own apps.

Watching recordings

Click an episode to play it in the browser; if you stopped partway, TabloView asks whether to resume or start over. Your place is saved to the Tablo, so the Tablo's own apps pick up where you left off too.

  • Timeline: hover it to see a preview picture of that moment, click to jump. Commercial breaks show as marks on it.
  • Speed: 1× to 1.5×, with the speed menu or the < and > keys.
  • Picture shape (the button showing Auto, or A): for channels that send a squeezed or stretched picture. Auto shows it as sent; 4:3 when people look too wide; 16:9 when they look too thin; Zoom for black bars on all four sides. It's remembered for that channel.
  • Captions (T), the sleep timer (the moon button) and picture in picture (P).
  • Open in VLC plays the recording in VLC instead.
  • Near the end, Up next offers the next episode.

Live TV

Live TV is a channel guide: click any channel to watch it. Antenna channels use one of your Tablo's tuners; streaming (FAST) channels don't.

  • Filters across the top narrow the guide: Favorites, Antenna, Streaming, and categories such as News or Sports.
  • Not enough tuners? If watching would leave too few tuners for a recording about to start, TabloView warns you first.
  • Pause and rewind up to 30 minutes; Home (or the LIVE button) jumps back to live.
  • Change channel with Page Up / Page Down, or C for the channel list beside the picture.
  • Record the show you're watching with the Record button, from now until it ends.
  • Signal meter: on antenna channels, a badge beside the channel name shows how much of the picture is arriving intact. Click it for the last 5 minutes.

Two channels at once: press 2 (or the two-rectangles button) and pick a second channel. Tab switches which one you hear, and V changes the layout: side by side, big and small, or one full screen. The silent one shows captions. Each channel uses its own tuner.

Recording shows

Find a show in the Guide, open it, and choose what to record; your Tablo does the recording, so TabloView doesn't need to be running.

  • Record New records only episodes the guide marks new; Record All records every airing; Stop ends the series.
  • One episode: choose Record on a single airing.
  • Browse: filter by TV shows, Movies or Sports (grouped by sport and league), or use the highlights such as new primetime episodes and premieres.
  • Streaming channels: the Streaming channels tab lists a week of shows on the free streaming channels, by category. Many of them can be recorded like antenna shows; the "Recordable channels only" filter shows which.

Scheduled lists every series you record and each upcoming recording:

  • Skip one upcoming episode without changing the series.
  • Won't record marks episodes your Tablo will miss because more shows overlap than it has tuners, so nothing goes missing quietly.
  • New episodes the Tablo won't record: some Tablos skip episodes the guide marks new in Record New series; these are listed with a Record button.
  • Add to calendar: add your upcoming recordings to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

If a recording fails or comes out short, TabloView can schedule a rerun automatically (Settings → General → Record failed recordings again).

Watch list

The Watch list plays recordings from any shows one after another, like a playlist, picking up where you left off in each.

  1. Add recordings with Add to watch list in a recording's ⋯ menu, or select several on a show's page and add them in one go.
  2. Open Watch list in the sidebar, reorder if you like, and press play.
  3. Choose Watch and keep, or Watch and delete to delete each recording from your Tablo once you've watched it to the end.

With Watch and delete, recordings you stop partway are kept, and protected recordings are never deleted. A recording comes off the list once it's watched to the end. Stopping halfway keeps your place, and the list stays as it is.

Skipping commercials and signal dropouts

TabloView finds the commercial breaks in your recordings on your PC, in the background, so you can skip them with one press or have them skipped automatically.

  1. On a show's page, turn on Skip commercials. TabloView finds the breaks in that show's unwatched recordings, newest first. For a single recording, use Find commercials in its ⋯ menu. For a series that hasn't recorded yet, the same switch is on its page in the Guide (or open it from Scheduled).
  2. Breaks appear as marks on the player's timeline. When one comes up, press Skip break (or S).
  3. To skip them without pressing anything, turn on Settings → Playback → Skip commercial breaks automatically.

It looks for the channel's logo in the corner of the picture (most networks take it off during ads), the moment of black and silence at the edges of a break, and how different the picture looks from the show. When it isn't sure, it leaves a few seconds of ad in rather than cut into the show, and it never skips past the end of the show.

Finding breaks takes a few minutes per recording and pauses while you watch or export, so it doesn't slow anything down. It keeps going with the browser closed, as long as TabloView is running.

Signal dropouts: when your Tablo says a recording didn't come in cleanly, TabloView finds where a weak signal broke up the picture and marks those parts in red on the timeline. Skip them with Skip dropout (or S), or automatically with Settings → Playback. Finding them means reading the whole recording once, in the background while nothing is playing: about a quarter of its length, using most of one CPU core. The status line shows Checking signal while it runs, and you can turn it off in Settings → Playback.

Watching on your TV and other devices

Other devices at home can use TabloView through a browser, while the PC running it does the work; that PC has to stay on.

Turn on network access (on the PC):

  1. Go to Settings → Desktop & network and turn on network access. If Windows Firewall asks, allow TabloView on Private networks.
  2. Set a PIN. Other devices enter it once and are remembered for 90 days; the PC itself never asks. After 5 wrong tries a device has to wait a minute, twice as long each time it happens again.
  3. Under the PIN, Devices using TabloView lists each device and when it was last used, and Show sign-ins lists sign-ins and wrong PINs. If you don't recognize something, change the PIN: every device has to sign in again.
  4. The same page shows the address for other devices, like http://192.168.1.50:7070.

Away from home, TabloView itself isn't on the internet, which keeps it safe, but a VPN to your home network gets you there. Tailscale is free and the simplest: install it on this PC and on your phone or laptop, signed in to the same account, and open http:// with the PC's Tailscale address and :7070, like http://100.101.102.103:7070 or http://mypc.tail1234.ts.net:7070. A VPN built into your router works too, with the usual home address. How smooth it is depends on your home internet's upload speed. Don't use Tailscale's Funnel, which puts TabloView on the open internet; with Serve or any other way of passing on requests, TabloView asks for the PIN as it does for other devices.

On a TV, use TV view: big screens for the remote. Its menu down the left side has Home (Continue watching, On now, Recently recorded, Recording soon, in the order chosen under Customize on the PC), Library, Live TV, your Watch list and Coming up. Select a recording to see what it's about, play it, add it to the Watch list, mark it watched, protect it or delete it. Live TV shows what's on now, or a Guide of the next 6 hours (OK on a later show records it). In live TV, Record in the buttons records what's on until it ends, and selecting a recording in Coming up skips it.

Google TV and Fire TV: the TabloView app. TV view as an app on the TV's home screen, moved around with the remote's arrows like any other app (no pointer to drag around). Installing it, once:

  1. Turn on the TV's developer mode. Google TV: Settings → System → About, then select Android TV OS build 7 times. Fire TV: Settings → My Fire TV → About, then select the first line (your device's name) 7 times.
  2. Install Downloader (by AFTVnews) from the TV's app store.
  3. In Downloader, enter this PC's address from Settings → Desktop & network with /app on the end, like 192.168.1.50:7070/app. If the TV says it can't install unknown apps, choose Settings, allow Downloader, and go back.
  4. Choose Install, then Open. The first time, it finds TabloView on your network by itself, asks whether that's your computer (by its name and address), then asks for the PIN.

Back goes back, then to the menu, then Home, and leaves the app from Home's menu. PC address at the bottom of the menu (or the remote's Menu button) changes which PC it uses. Updating TabloView on the PC updates what the app shows; the app itself rarely needs reinstalling.

Other TVs, or without the app: use the TV's browser. On Google TV install TV Bro from the Play Store; on Fire TV use Amazon Silk. Most TV browsers move a pointer around with the remote's arrows.

  • Open the address with /tv on the end, typing http:// too, or the browser searches for it instead. From then on that browser opens in TV view.
  • Arrows move, OK picks, Back goes back. ◀ from the left edge (or Back) opens the menu.
  • While watching, OK brings up big buttons: Pause, 10 s back, 30 s ahead, CC (captions), Shape and Close (live TV: Back to live and Record). Without them, ◀ ▶ skip and ▲ ▼ change channel.
  • When a recording ends, Up next starts the show's next unwatched episode in a few seconds (OK plays it now, Back stops). Turning off Play the next episode automatically on the Settings page, in the same browser, turns it off.
  • Commercial breaks show on the progress bar and are skipped as on the PC: automatically if that's on, otherwise press OK during a break to skip it.
  • PC view (at the bottom of TV view's menu) switches that browser back to the full TabloView, and TV view (top right on the PC) switches to TV view.
  • Leaving the TV's browser with the Home button pauses what's playing, and closes it after 30 seconds away so the tuner is freed.

Roku has no usable browser. Most Rokus can show the PC's screen instead: press Win+K on the PC and pick the Roku.

Saving recordings to your PC

An export saves a recording as an MP4 file on your PC, which keeps it even if your Tablo is replaced or the recording is deleted.

  • Choose Export MP4 in a recording's ⋯ menu, or select several episodes and export them together.
  • Automatically, for a show: turn on Export to MP4 on the show's page. Each new recording is exported once it has finished recording, and you can choose to export the ones already recorded too. They're exported one at a time, each only once (deleting the MP4 or cancelling it under Downloads doesn't bring it back), and they wait while the drive has less than 10 GB free. This works while TabloView is running; recordings made while the PC is off are exported the next time it starts.
  • Follow them on the Downloads page, where you can cancel or remove one.
  • Exports run several times faster than real time while nothing else plays from your Tablo, and slow down by themselves when something starts, so watching isn't affected.

Using Plex, Jellyfin or Kodi? Point a TV Shows library at your exports folder and turn on Show and season folders. For Plex, sign in under Settings → Plex and TabloView asks your Plex server to scan each export as soon as it's saved (otherwise Plex often doesn't notice new files on a network drive until its next scheduled scan). Once Plex has it, the episode is marked watched there if you'd watched the recording (or resumes where you stopped), and if Plex matched it to the wrong show, TabloView matches it to the right one. A show's page marks the recordings Plex has already (In Plex) and asks before exporting one again, and its Delete once in Plex switch deletes each recording from your Tablo once its export is in Plex (protected and incomplete recordings are kept). Each export also has its title, show, season and episode, description and air date written into the MP4 itself, which players and Windows (the file's Properties → Details) show.

Questions and troubleshooting

Does closing the browser stop TabloView? No. It keeps running in the tray, so other devices, background syncs and commercial finding keep working. To stop it, right-click the tray icon and choose Quit. On a Mac or Linux, use Settings → Desktop & network → Stop TabloView, or press Ctrl+C in its Terminal window.

How do I update? The Microsoft Store version updates itself. The installer version shows Update available when there's a new one; you can also check from the tray icon. On a Mac or Linux, unzip the new version over the old folder and start it as usual; your sign-in and settings are kept.

Playback pauses or stutters. First, test the network: Settings → Desktop & network → Test connection says how fast video arrives from your Tablo, and the Wi-Fi signal of the Tablo (if it says) and of this computer. If that's fine, the PC is the likely cause: it converts the video as you watch, so an older PC can fall behind. In Settings → Playback, try Video quality Fastest, turn Smooth motion off, and choose a longer Buffer before playing. The line under Video quality says whether your graphics chip is doing the work.

The picture looks squeezed, stretched or boxed in. Use Picture shape in the player (or A). It's remembered for that channel.

"Tablo isn't responding." Check the Tablo is on and on the same network as the PC. TabloView keeps trying and carries on by itself when the Tablo answers again (and follows the Tablo if it gets a new address). If it never clears while Tablo's own apps work:

  1. Open the Tablo's address from the Settings page in your browser with /ping on the end, like http://192.168.1.164:8887/ping. A working Tablo answers at once with a short line like {"sid":"SID_…"}.
  2. No answer: this PC can't reach the Tablo at all. Pause any VPN (or allow "LAN" access in its settings), and check the PC is on the same network as the Tablo, not a guest Wi-Fi.
  3. It answers, but TabloView still can't connect: security software with its own firewall may be blocking TabloView; allow it there.
  4. Still stuck on Windows: open Command Prompt as administrator, run netsh int ip reset, and restart the PC. It resets Windows' network settings, which clears out leftovers from old VPNs or virtual network adapters that can send traffic for your home network nowhere (a user's fix).

A channel won't tune. All tuners may be busy with recordings or another app: click Tuners at the top of the page to see. If it still fails, the Tablo may not be picking up that channel right now; the Antenna helper shows its signal.

Live TV stopped. TabloView restarts a stuck stream by itself; if it can't, press Try again.

Will I lose my recordings if my Tablo is replaced? Recordings belong to the Tablo that made them, so a replacement starts empty, even with the same drive. Export the ones you want to keep (see Saving recordings to your PC).

Still stuck? Copy the details from Settings → Diagnostics and report the problem on GitHub.
